What Makes This Different

Not a textbook. Not a policy doc.

Written like Richard Preston's The Hot Zone crossed with a true-crime podcast. You'll learn more about insider threats from these stories than from any certification course.

🎯

Cold Opens

Every chapter drops you into a moment of crisis. No preamble. No context. Just a person making the worst decision of their life.

🧠

Psychology First

The MICE framework — Money, Ideology, Compromise, Ego. Understand why trusted people betray, and you'll see the warning signs before the damage hits.

⚡

Real Scale

$2.6 billion in stolen IP. 700,000 classified documents. An entire country's intelligence assets burned. These aren't hypotheticals.

🕵️

State-Level Tradecraft

China's Thousand Talents. North Korea's ghost workforce. Russia's 22-year mole. Nation-state insider operations dissected in detail.

💻

Technical Depth

UEBA systems, behavioral analytics, zero-trust architecture — explained through the stories of the breaches that demanded them.

About the Author

Ian Trimble

Ian Trimble is a cybersecurity instructor and practitioner who has spent years training professionals to detect, prevent, and respond to the threats that keep CISOs awake at night.

With experience spanning incident response, threat intelligence, and security architecture, Ian brings the rare combination of technical depth and narrative skill needed to tell these stories the way they deserve to be told — as thrillers that happen to be true.

The Insider is his second book, following the neo-noir cyberpunk novel The Halcyon Key.
